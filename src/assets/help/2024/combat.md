# Combat Quick Reference (PHB 2024)

## Turn structure
On your turn you can move up to your speed and take one action. You may also use one bonus action and one reaction per round (reaction refreshes at the start of your next turn).

## Actions
Common actions: Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object. Some features grant extra attacks or bonus actions.

## Attack rolls
d20 + ability modifier + proficiency (if proficient) vs target AC. Natural 20 is a critical hit; natural 1 misses. Roll damage dice twice on a crit.

## Advantage and disadvantage
Roll 2d20 and take the higher (advantage) or lower (disadvantage). Multiple sources do not stack; if you have both, they cancel.

## Cover
Half cover +2 AC/Dex saves; three-quarters cover +5; total cover cannot be targeted directly.

## Weapon Mastery
Martial classes assign mastery properties (Cleave, Graze, Nick, Push, Sap, Slow, Topple, Vex) to qualified weapons. Only one mastery per weapon unless a feature says otherwise.

## Death saves
At 0 HP, roll d20 each turn: 10+ success, 9 or below failure. Three successes stabilize; three failures die. Nat 20: regain 1 HP. Nat 1: two failures.

## Rests
Short rest: spend Hit Dice to heal; many features recharge on short rest. Long rest: regain all HP, half Hit Dice, and spell slots; features that recharge daily reset.

## Heroic Inspiration
You can have Heroic Inspiration from the DM or from your species (e.g. Human). Spend it to reroll any d20 after rolling, or give it to another creature (Human). You must use the new roll.
