# Skills & Exploration Quick Reference (PHB 2024)

## Ability checks
d20 + ability modifier + proficiency (if proficient). The DM sets the DC. Group checks: everyone rolls; half or more succeed.

## Saving throws
Resist spells, traps, and effects. d20 + ability modifier + proficiency if you have save proficiency.

## Skills by ability
STR: Athletics. DEX: Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth. INT: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion. WIS: Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival. CHA: Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion.

## Passive Perception
10 + Wisdom modifier + proficiency if proficient in Perception. Used to notice hidden creatures or traps without rolling.

## Tool checks
Use the ability the DM specifies (often relevant ability for the tool). Add proficiency if proficient with that tool.

## Social interaction
Roleplay first; use Persuasion, Deception, or Intimidation when the outcome is uncertain. Insight opposes deception.

## Exploration
Travel pace affects Perception and Stealth. Stealth group checks use the lowest roll. Searching an area may use Investigation or Perception per DM.

## Heroic Inspiration
Renamed from Inspiration in 2014. The DM can award it for good play. Spend to reroll a d20 test (attack, check, or save) after rolling; use the new roll.

## Terminology
Species (not race) and Origin (background + species details) are the 2024 character-building terms. Background grants ability increases and an Origin feat at level 1.
